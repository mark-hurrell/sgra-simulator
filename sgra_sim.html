All positions are in astronomical units relative to Sagittarius A star.

The intruder is created at the initial position. Its initial velocity is calculated from the velocity reference point toward the initial position, so it initially travels away from the reference point.

These are illustrative starting configurations. What follows depends on the whole system and is not predicted here.

Accessibility & diagnostics
Keyboard shortcuts

When the simulation view has focus: Arrow keys rotate the camera; + or - zooms; H returns to Home; E selects Earth view; O aligns the orbit plane; Space plays or pauses; R resets; F follows; T toggles trails; L toggles labels; C toggles the cusp; [ and ] adjust speed; ? opens this help.

During Group Follow, the camera follows the encounter; arrows or drag adjust the chase view and +/− or scroll adjusts zoom.

Placement mode uses a drag to aim and launch. Press Escape to exit placement.
